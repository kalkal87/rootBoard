# Findings: Jobs to Be Done

## Framework
- **Name:** Jobs to Be Done
- **Slug:** jobs-to-be-done
- **Lens summary:** Identifies the underlying job a customer is "hiring"
  the product to do, and where the product currently fails to do that job.

## Observations
- Customers ask support to perform bulk edits over email because they can't
  find a way to do it themselves in the console (CS manager, ~9:00).
- Support already performs manual bulk edits for about 20% of accounts,
  informally, when an account escalates (CS manager, ~16:45).
- Three customers mentioned they almost churned because the renewal process
  took so long to sort out manually (Support lead, ~19:10).

## Assumptions
- Assuming the "job" here is specifically about renewal-deadline pressure
  (getting access changes done before a fixed date), not a general desire
  for bulk edit as a nice-to-have — every mention of this need in the input
  is tied to the renewal date, not casual admin work.

## Open Questions
- Do accounts outside the white-glove 20% attempt to self-serve and give
  up, or do they not ask at all? The input only shows the customers who
  escalated loudly enough to be noticed.

## Problems Identified

### Problem 1: The job "get my team's access sorted before my renewal deadline, in one action" has no self-serve path
- **Problem Statement:** Enterprise admins are hiring the product to let
  them finish bulk access changes before a fixed renewal deadline, and the
  only way that job currently gets done is an inconsistent, manual
  workaround performed by support for whichever accounts escalate loudly
  enough.
- **Impact / Symptoms:** Three customers nearly churned; human labor fulfills
  the job for only about 20% of accounts.
- **Proof Points:**
  - "Customers keep asking us to just do the bulk edit for them over email
    because they can't find it in the console." (CS manager, ~9:00)
  - "We give white-glove manual bulk edits to about 20% of accounts
    already, informally, when someone escalates loud enough." (CS manager,
    ~16:45)
  - "Three customers mentioned they almost churned because the renewal
    process took so long to sort out manually." (Support lead, ~19:10)
- **Confidence:** High

## Coverage Note
Jobs to Be Done fit this input strongly — the need is repeatedly framed
around a deadline-driven task, which is exactly what this lens is built to
surface. High confidence reflects three independent, concrete proof points
rather than a single passing mention.
